How it works step 4 fallback vs gate 'Honest prices': Canadian Tire is always blocked by the extension, so its prices always come from the fallback, and search-snippet prices are often stale. Before searching, try WebFetch on the store page. A price taken from a snippet is labelled 'from search, not verified on the store page', linked to where it came from, and never used alone for the Answer's 'where to buy'. The Honest prices gate counts only prices verified on the store page.
