WindowShopper

You name something you want to buy. It reads trusted reviews for the models that fit your needs, compares their Canadian prices and price history, narrows to a shortlist, and adds a report that explains what to buy and where to one private page holding all your reports, readable on any device. Just for you. Runs in Claude Code, from your computer or your phone. It never buys anything.

How it works

  1. Ask. You say find: <item> and whatever matters to you: budget, size, must-haves, deal-breakers.
  2. Questions. It asks only where your answer would change the picks and it can't be confident what you'd say. Each comes with a recommendation; "your call" takes it. Often there are none.
  3. Longlist. It reads reviews from the sources below and keeps every model that fits your needs, is well reviewed and is sold new in Canada: enough that price can still change the answer, often 5 to 8.
  4. Prices. For each longlist model, current prices in CAD at the stores that fit the item, each with its link, stock and the time checked. Price history where it can find it, and whether now is a good time to buy. It reads store and tracker pages in your Chrome through Claude in Chrome, so stores see a normal browser. On each site it first declines non-essential cookies and closes pop-ups. A price counts only for the exact model, new, with the seller named (store or marketplace seller) and what's included; one model can be listed at very different prices on the same store. If Chrome isn't reachable, a store refuses it (the extension blocks some, such as Canadian Tire's sites) or a page won't load, it first tries WebFetch on the store page, then search results. A price it can't read on the store page is labelled "from search, not verified", linked to where it came from, and never used alone for the Answer.
  5. Shortlist. With reviews and prices side by side, it narrows to up to 3: best overall, best value, and one alternative when it is worth knowing. Fewer is fine when one model clearly wins.
  6. Report. Added at the top of reports/page.html (made from templates/page.html on the first find), with its date and item in the page's contents list. The session publishes the page with the Artifact tool and sends you the link. On the first publish it writes the page's claude.ai URL into reports/page.html as <meta name="artifact-url">; every later find or recheck passes that URL to Artifact, so the same page is updated, never a new one. The page is titled "WindowShopper reports".
  7. Recheck. recheck <report> refreshes the prices and price history of a past report, in place on the page, with the new check time, and publishes the page again.

The report

One page holds every report, newest first, under a contents list of date, item and top pick. It is readable on a phone. Each report has the parts below, in order; templates/page.html holds the shape.

  1. Answer. What to buy and where, in two or three lines.
  2. What you asked for. Your needs, and anything it assumed.
  3. The shortlist. A comparison table, then each pick: why it fits you, what reviewers praise and criticise, who it is not for.
  4. Where to buy. Per pick: store, seller, price, stock, link, time checked; price history and whether to buy now or wait.
  5. Left out. The rest of the longlist and popular models it rejected, one line each with why, price included.
  6. Sources. Every review and page it used, linked.
